THE NINEFOLD VEIL — FACTION COMPANION CHARACTER PROMPT
Version 1.0 — Draft for approval

Create an original companion character belonging to one of the four established factions in The Ninefold Veil: Jeoseung Saja, Dokkaebi, Gumiho, or Sansin. Follow the approved 2D hybrid art direction: pixel-art-based in-game characters, layered 2D environments, and rich but readable combat effects.

Do not use “webtoon style” as the defining visual direction for in-game assets. Do not assume a 3D pipeline or engine change.

CHARACTER DESIGN
- The character must have a distinct silhouette that remains recognizable at gameplay size.
- Use a distinctive hairstyle or head shape, readable costume outline, and one signature ornament or faction emblem.
- Include a primary weapon or combat accessory only when appropriate to the approved character concept.
- Use the shared blue-black / charcoal, jade / muted teal, and vermilion palette, with a restrained faction-specific accent if useful.
- Show faction identity through silhouette, costume structure, accessory, markings, and iconography—not color alone.
- Reinterpret Korean folklore and traditional motifs in an original fantasy vocabulary.
- Avoid excessive surface detail, tiny ornaments, generic European medieval armor, photorealistic textures, and details that become noise in a small sprite.

FACTION VISUAL DIRECTIONS
- Jeoseung Saja: death, passage, records, spiritual authority.
- Dokkaebi: bold energy, supernatural power, mischievous or forceful personality.
- Gumiho: illusion, elegance, fox symbolism, supernatural intelligence.
- Sansin: mountains, nature, spiritual guardianship, ancient authority.
These are visual directions, not fixed stereotypes. Do not invent gameplay abilities, narrative relationships, or backstory unless separately provided.

PRESENTATION AND SPRITE
- For a reference sheet, show the complete character on a plain background, including important accessories.
- The in-game version must be designed as a pixel-art sprite for the 2D hybrid game—not merely a resized illustration.
- Character proportion is not restricted to 2.5–3 heads; approximately 2-head proportions are allowed (user decision, 2026-10-28). The nominal 48 × 48 logical-pixel size is a preliminary reference, not a final requirement.
- Preserve the hairstyle/head silhouette, main costume shape, faction identity, and signature accessory.
- Simplify detail for readability and frame-based animation.
- Transparent background for isolated assets. No UI, labels, logos, decorative frames, or unrelated scenery.

Do not assume all companions have identical body proportions or weapons. Maintain a shared art language while preserving each character’s individual silhouette.
