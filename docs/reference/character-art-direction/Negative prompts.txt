NOTE
These are general exclusions, not a substitute for asset-specific instructions. Smooth glow and layered 2D lighting effects are allowed when they support the approved hybrid direction and preserve gameplay readability. Do not treat this prompt as a ban on all atmospheric backgrounds or all overhead visibility. The target is an angled top-down quarter-view with readable combat, not a rigid mathematical-isometric requirement.
